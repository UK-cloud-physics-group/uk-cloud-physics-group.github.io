UK Early-Career Cloud Physics Group

Resources

This collection of resources was predominantly produced for the UK ECR Cloud Physics Group, so contributions from other areas of weather and climate sciences are greatly welcomed.

General weather and climate in plain language
NOAA blog

Plain-language articles from NOAA explaining climate science and current climate events. No longer maintained, but the archive remains a useful resource.

Climate.us (started up by ex-Climate.gov employees)

An independent, non-profit home for the plain-language climate news, data and teaching resources formerly hosted by Climate.gov.

Escape from model land

Erica Thompson's accessible guide to how mathematical models shape our view of the world, and how to recognise when they are leading us astray.

What does rain smell like?

Meteorologists Simon King and Clare Nasir answer curious questions about the weather and how to read the sky, in an accessible question-and-answer format.

Blue machine

Helen Czerski explores the ocean as a vast engine, showing how currents, tides and waves store and move energy around the planet.

Data sources
Coupled climate model data

Metagrid, the search portal for CMIP6 and other climate model output held by the Earth System Grid Federation — the archive that underpins IPCC assessments.

List of satellites and descriptions

An overview of past and present Earth observation satellites and the instruments they carry, with links to mission pages.

Textbooks
Global Physical Climatology

Dennis L. Hartmann's graduate-level textbook on the physical processes governing Earth's climate, from radiation and the energy budget to ocean-atmosphere coupling. ISBN: 978-0-433-27331-5.

An Introduction to Atmospheric Physics

David G. Andrews' concise undergraduate introduction to the physics of the atmosphere, covering radiation, thermodynamics, dynamics and the middle atmosphere.

An introduction to clouds

Ulrike Lohmann and colleagues' textbook on cloud physics, taking the reader from the microscale of droplets and ice crystals up to the role of clouds in climate.

Python packages
xarray

Round-up of packages built on xarray, the standard Python library for labelled N-dimensional arrays, which is central to working with gridded geoscience data.

pySat

The Python Satellite Data Analysis Toolkit: a common interface for downloading, loading, cleaning and analysing satellite and other space-science data.

csat2

A library for reading and processing observational meteorological data, with a focus on satellite datasets. It smooths over differing file naming conventions, so the same code runs on different machines and datasets.

metpy

A Python toolkit for reading, visualising and analysing meteorological data, including standard plots such as Skew-T diagrams and a rich collection of thermodynamic and kinematic calculations.
