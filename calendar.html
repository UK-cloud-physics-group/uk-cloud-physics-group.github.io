UK Early-Career Cloud Physics Group

Calendar

This is a collection of upcoming events with potential application to cloud physics, including workshops and trainings that aren't cloud-focused. If you have a suggested addition, send it to one of the current chairs (Mathilde or Kallista).

If you have missed a registration deadline, it is always worth emailing the organisers and asking politely whether late registration is possible, especially for events like hackathons where the organisers want active participants.

2026

September 28–30: 2026 Aerosol, Cloud, Precipitation, and Climate (ACPC) Workshop in Hamburg, Germany, and online. Abstract submission deadline July 31.

October 12–16: APARC (Atmospheric Processes And their Role in Climate) General Assembly 2026, Pune (India). Abstract submission deadline May 15.

October 14–16: The 8th International Electronic Conference on Atmospheric Sciences, online. Abstract deadline June 21. Registration deadline October 7.

November 11, 15:00 UK time: European Meteorological Society Webinar: Atmospheric Science from Satellites – Focus on Trace Gases and Aerosol Effects on Climate, online.
